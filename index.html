Goomba Grails

Goomba Grails is my vintage and Y2K clothing brand. I started it my sophomore year of high school in Vancouver, Washington, and spent years hunting, flipping, and selling.

Over the years, I sold on Depop, eBay, and Instagram and at vintage markets around Spokane and Coeur d'Alene. On Depop alone, I sold over 1,284 pieces, earned 303 reviews and built 803 followers, all with next-day shipping. These days school at Gonzaga comes first, but I still keep an eye out for the next grail.
